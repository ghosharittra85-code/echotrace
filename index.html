EchoTrace Clarity before trust. The same patient can echo across the training and test sets of a medical imaging dataset. EchoTrace finds them, even after patient IDs are deleted, and fixes the split.

Is the test patient already in the training set?

One scan session makes many near-identical images of one patient. If they are split carelessly, a model is tested on someone it has already seen, and its score measures memory, not diagnosis. EchoTrace compares the images themselves and flags pairs too similar to come from different patients.

Or drag a folder or a .zip file onto this page.

Everything runs on this computer. No image is uploaded, copied or sent anywhere.

How to arrange the folder

Check a split
Put images in folders named train and test (or val), at any depth. Without them, EchoTrace splits the images itself the usual way (80/20 at random, image by image) and shows what that split would leak.
Memorisation score
Put each class in its own folder inside them, for example train/benign.
Check accuracy
Give each patient a folder, or name files like PATIENT001_scan2.png.
Formats
PNG, JPEG, BMP, WebP, GIF and TIFF where the browser supports it. Masks named _mask or _gt are skipped.
